---
Title: "Highway"
---

Order and law turn a blind eye

a beggar in rags, a stranger in tie

The cloth of justice covers her eyes

the burden of truth, the burden of lies

She cannot see, this through my eyes

She stares down me, looks through my lies

A kiln is the fire of birth

even though she moulds with mirth

the edges, the corners get smoothened out

the parts of my life hidden by my clout

my mind is riddled with doubt

how does she reach my heart,

the highway route?
