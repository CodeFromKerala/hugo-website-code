## Sand Me Down

Whispered questions

Whispered answers

Can't pick myself apart

Real me from the cancers

She's one of the dancers

Wondering where are my manners

Saying without doing is pretending

Gotta be clear, what message I'm sending 

Have to, need to

Preserve the beauty of my words

My paragraphs

My sentences

Fixing everything that's wrong

Guided by a loving hand

Find the strength to sand

Away the uneven land

Invaluable is the trust that is given

Lost is the trust I've been handed

Will I stop hating myself

If to the core I'm sanded?
