---
Title: "Entropy"
---

Entropy stares at order
wondering where it all went wrong

Entropy fucking hates order
but looks at his life with envy

Why can't I make planets?
Why can't I make comets?
Why doesn't the universe want me to flourish?

Little did entropy know

That the universe tries to have more of her
in his life

that she is everything the universe needs

The large mountains, the large lakes
but unfortunately none of it makes
past entropy my dear

and entropy doesn't want the universe to leave either

Will entropy know how much the universe needs her?
Will the universe know how much entropy needs him?

Only time will tell
