---
Title: Fractional Calculus
---

Fractional Calculus is a branch of calculus that deals with derivatives of real number order.

For example, a normal derivative is of integer order,
$$ \frac{d}{dx},\frac{d^2}{dx^2},\frac{d^3}{dx^3} $$

But a fractional derivative uses real number order,
$$ \frac{d^{0.5}}{dx^{0.5}}, \frac{d^\pi}{dx^\pi}, \frac{d^e}{dx^e}$$
