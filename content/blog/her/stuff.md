Title: "Stuff"
---

It hurts so much that I can't call her anything I want anymore. jaan, ji, princess, keerthana. I can't say what's on my mind. 

Atleast she doesn't know that I'm hurt.

It's true, you can never keep choosing somebody who doesn't choose you back. I'm sure she's just keeping me around till either she falls in love, which is unlikely, or she moves on. I'm just gonna end up a side character.

I wish I could stay friends. But, I sacrificed that long ago.

I need to decide whether this is worth fighting for or not.

But how can I do that when I haven't seen enough ? We went into all of this so fast. I thought I learnt something from my past disasters but I obviously haven't.

I just wished that she loved me as much, but sometimes you don't get what you wished for.

I wish I could write more, but I don't know what to write at this point. I don't know which feelings to express, which feelings to hide.

I just want my princess back.
