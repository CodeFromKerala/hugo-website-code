My Cup

I have a cup And a desperate need to fill it

I don’t know why tho everyone else wants to just do it

It seems impressive How do they carry the cup without spilling? How do they do the process of filling?

I started finding ways to fill the cup And the options were many I just started to hoard them in Pouring from every jug I found

I never stopped to ask And I never asked to stop

Is what I’m pouring in the cup good for me? Do I want it in my cup? Or do I want everyone to know that I’m up?

I never stopped to ask And I never asked to stop

Now, I found this person, with a cup to the brim of things I like She took a look inside my cup and what she said is worse than having my head on a pike

You haven’t learnt anything, have you? She asked

I hadn’t thought for myself

I thought I just needed the cup full

But the cup was never meant to be full

The cup had to hold everything I wanted to give, yet now the cup is against my will to live

Still, the cup is mine, and mine alone.

I have to change the contents of the cup alone

Well on, I said, and started to fix I didn’t know the cup had started to mix

The wrong stuff hadn’t been sitting, they’ve been mixing and they’ve been worsening

The cup had begun to hold things I hadn’t put in

The person I found had to suffer from my cup a lot The hurt I’ve caused can’t be forgotten

But I learn from my mistakes, I’ve learnt to ask

I learnt to stop to ask I learnt to ask to stop

I have to fill my cup with the remainder of the sunlight and rain, pour out the vanity and pain

With the cup, nobody is sane, nobody is exactly the same

I just pray that my cup will never hurt anyone again
