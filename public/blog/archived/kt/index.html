I deleted all of kt’s chat like the impulsive asshole I am.

I made so many mistakes - time to analyse them then.

Date: 12-08-2026

Kt is the sweetest and nicest girl I’ve ever met. I fell for her immediately, which I suspect is for her passion (and slight crazy nature).

But as always, I can’t be trusted with good things. I messed up, I was careless and never checked up on her. Ever since deciding to give “this” a shot, I’ve been slacking.

It hurts me so much to know that she was hurt by something I did. I wanted to be so much more better for her. I was absent when she needed reassurance the most. I’d give anything for a second chance.

I never knew she wanted us to work that bad. I feel selfish saying this, but I wish she had let me know of my shortcomings at the moment I made them, but the nature of my mistakes are different. I never made it truly clear that I loved her.

My words don’t mean jack now. I want us to be a thing, and by thing I mean a relationship. I was never clear. I never clarified all my thoughts about her. I thought I had time.

Her eyes are jet black. She hates messaging on insta cus it doesn’t let her track and remember the small things (never told her how much I like it). Her favorite color is red, and her second favorite is black. She’d rather go to the mountains than the beach (even though she loves both). The most important person in her life, the person she’s living for, is her brother. I remember that they share a 10 year age gap. I still remember the huge amount of respect for her with how she answered my questions on god. I remember that she actively recalled that and wanted to help me reach a solution or stable state on that topic of belief as well. Somewhere along the way, I stopped being myself. I stopped being that man she wanted.

I used really insensitive words. I missed her insanely obvious cues. I still want to know more about her brother. I want to write paragraphs for her.

I setup this whole elaborate bs where I told her not to call me baby until she felt the same about me. I think she called me something along those lines in the last few days, but I forgot to bring it up.

I wish we could’ve actually talked more. I wanted to talk to her one night but she was occupied - I was so sad, I made this whole mental situation where I confront her about what “this” is but I messed up big time.

No one has ever even come close to knowing me this much. I was, to a point, ignorant about her trauma. I tried to play it safe, and then ended up sending insensitive stuff in less than 5 hours. I should think with my stupid brain before talking to people I love.

I’m still confused about how she feels. I’m pretty sure its anger towards me (and herself probably). I know even she is confused by her feelings. I just wish I knew she wanted me the same as I wanted her.

I never regretted meeting you. I’ve never regretted staying. I don’t think I ever will cus you have brought so much happiness in my life in such a short amount of time.

I forgot how well I used to sleep, knowing you would talk to me again in the morning.
