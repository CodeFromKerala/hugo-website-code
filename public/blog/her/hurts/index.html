It hurts.

I never have the guts to say this directly to her. It hurt from the beginning that she didn’t love me as much as I love her. It hurt from the beginning that despite giving it my all, fate still didn’t bless me with someone who loves me the same. I thought, after the no contact break that she actually had real, deep feelings for me but it’s never love. I don’t think it’ll ever be love. I don’t think I can be enough for her definition of love.

I don’t know what to do. I definitely don’t want to hurt her by leaving her alone. The way she’s talking, I’m really not hopeful that this goes anywhere. I should’ve known from the start, I’ll never be enough. The time, place and situation are all against me. I don’t think I have it in me to keep fighting much longer. But saying that out loud makes me lose hope, and that’s about the only thing I have.

Most probably, this will end the way it always does. We decide to split ways. We decide that it’s not healthy. We go on seperate paths.

I have no idea what I’m gonna do without her. I don’t know what to do with this pain in my chest. I don’t know what to do with the tears welling up.

It’s gonna take a miracle to save this - and that’s just the hope talking.

The helplessness I feel right now, I wouldn’t wish on my worst enemy.

But I gotta stick to my principles, I’ve been given this heart to love and I won’t let it go to waste. Anything I feel like doing, I’ll do. Anything I feel like saying, I’ll say. It’s gonna hurt at the end, but It’s better than to never have done nothing at all. I couldn’t possibly forgive myself if I didn’t give it my all.
