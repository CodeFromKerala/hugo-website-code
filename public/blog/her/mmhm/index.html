Math. Programming. Shithousery.

Impossible to comprehend

only things that I wish to lend

are my eyes, jaan, to make you see

how beautiful I see you to be

.

A jester can’t be forced to play

this game of love, he isn’t forced to stay

an atheist once, he can’t help but pray

I don’t want anything or anything but bae

.

Un-starry nights, darkness galore

I haven’t even seen her before

didn’t even know the night in store

rather see the stars in your eyes, forevermore

.

adorn in blood, cold as the night

warmth in me couldn’t put up a fight

In her arms, it feels so light

needing her near, missing her with all my might

.

All these voices, can’t bring myself

to leave, can’t tell myself

to throw away with both hands

the only place where “me” stands

.

The only hues the sky can make

only so much my heart can take

I can’t think with so much at stake

I have to be sincere, how can I be fake?

.

and so it goes

not lows, not foes

fear hath made the mind clear

companionship or face my fear
