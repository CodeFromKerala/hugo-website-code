Dear Keerthana,

I love you.

I love you so much that I’ve begun to wonder whether I should be feeling this way. I love how you think. I love how you talk. I love your voice, your eyes, your beautiful mathanga ahh head.

My biggest reason for loving you is because I’m myself around you, and I’ve never shared that with anyone so it’s very scary and I don’t talk about it as much.

You’re not dumb, and you’re not a loser. Having come all this way and still fighting till the next day just proves that you’re a fighter and I love that. I love you.

Well, princess, I unfortunately don’t drop hidden meanings in my texts. Anything I wanna say, will be said if it’s a comfortable enough environment.

Now, about the running out of topics thing, we are definitely gonna run into that. The only thing is that we keep this space clear enough that we can talk about stuff more than once. We definitely do lack overlap, but I personally don’t think that it’s necessary for love. I should be able to love a person I have nothing in similar to, just for the way they are. And that’s the kind of love I have for you.

No matter what you are, or what you once were, I’ll still love you.

I do. I love you so much. You matter so much to me that I can’t even put it in words yet.

The processing time is a real constraint but the thing is that I operate more efficiently when we actually talk - that makes me feel present, unlike texting - I usually think and give you responses that you’d need.

Your love language is beautiful. It’s not a chore, it’s not a burden. Even if it will feel so, it’s worth it, cus it’s you. I will give it my all to love you in all the ways you wanna be loved and only thing I want to ask is for you to be patient with me and tell me when you feel neglected or not loved.

Yes. I do see us working out. I see us helping each other reach heights and resting on each other’s shoulders when the world gets too much. I want to be that person for you. I wanna be yours. (Yes, arctic monkeys’ reference, I know)

Love is a work in progress, na jaan ? I feel as though if we work at it we can both make each other happier than we’ve ever felt before.

Thank you so much for being honest with me. It means a lot.

It’s definitely not fucking easy to leave you. I don’t want to leave you. I don’t even love myself enough - why the fuck do I love you for? I just never want to leave you alone. I’ve had nightmares of you all alone. Nightmares of you not being okay and me leaving you alone. It’s the worst thing I could do. So, never ever say that it’s easy to leave for me. Staying here with you makes my heart full.

I love you so much

Everything will be fine, jaanu. I promise.

I’ll be there to catch you when you fall. I’ll be there to help you back to your feet.

I love you so much. There’s nothing else I want more than you loving me back.

I love you so much, keerthana. I could say it a million times.

I love you, keerthana - to the moon and back.

Yours, with love,

Madhav
