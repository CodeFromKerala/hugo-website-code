I think chris here is trying to say that moving on or letting go becomes easy only when you’ve seen all that that relationship can become, or you achieve some kind of closure.

“Holding onto a thread” is he talking about holding onto a small memory? Is that why it’s difficult for him to move on?

“A kiss is better when you give it back”, yep I think he’s clarifying that reciprocated love is the only love that’s worth something. Might be wrong but I don’t agree with the statement, of course.

“He constantly leaves us”, talking about dad?

“Competing in love”, “Wish we were enough”, signs of attaching your self worth to the whims of someone you love.

So fool’s gold is just unreciprocated love ?
