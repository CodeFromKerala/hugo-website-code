Math. Programming. Shithousery.

Dear keerthana,

I admire you. I respect you.

And importantly, I love you.

You’re still my kanmani, I’m just a bit farther away than usual.

When you said that you loved me yesterday, I was on a bus heading back home. I burst into tears at reading the message but luckily there weren’t many people around to hear it. I cried again before I sat on call with you.

Believe me, it hurts. But the thought of hurting you more is unbearable.

I want you to be happy. But selfishly, I want you to be happy with me.

I promise to be a better person for you at the end of this. Somebody you can trust to call your boyfriend. Somebody you can love without fear.

I don’t care if you’re mine right now. But I’m yours and yours alone.

Madhav
