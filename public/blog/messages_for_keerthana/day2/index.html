9:10

Hey, sweetheart,

Last night I had a dream which went something like this.

I was standing at pattom bus stop, waiting for the kulathoor bus, my favorite, to take me to college.

I got on, paid for my ticket, and saw a seat, the only free one, on the aisle.

I look to my side, and at the window seat is madhav, and next to me there’s you. You’re hugging madhav’s arm, and I could feel my chest tighten.

But then I realise, that ain’t madhav at all. It’s some man with some face, who I don’t know.

and I’m not next to you, I’m observing from outside the bus.

I try to desperately climb onto the bus, but the conductor says they are full.

I just wait for the next bus.

9:20

I cried a lot after waking up, just thinking about that. I couldn’t bear imagining you being happy with someone else, as utmostly selfish as that is.

But I do want you to be happy, even without me.

I hope you live a very happy life, jaanu. I hope you excel in everything you do, meet excellent people, and live a good life.

Thank you for showing me how love feels. Thank you for loving me. Thank you for letting me love you.
